The Recipe

Four steps turn "who matches whom" into a real answer — in plain words, then the formula as one labeled reference.

A recipe is just steps

You already know the idea from the last module: each word makes a Query (what it wants), a Key (what it advertises), and a Value (what it carries).

But making those three things is not the answer yet. We still have to use them. That is what this module is about: the four simple steps that turn all those matches into a fresh meaning for each word.

🎯
The whole module in one breath

SCORE every word against every word. SHRINK the scores a little. Turn them into percentages with softmax. BLEND the payloads by those percentages. Four steps. That is the recipe.

Mixing paint to a target shade

Imagine you want a very specific shade of orange. You have jars of red, yellow, and a little blue. You do not pour them in randomly. You decide how much of each color to add based on how well that color helps you reach the shade you want.

🎯

Your target shade

The exact orange you are aiming for. This is the word's Query — what it is looking for.

🏷️

Each jar's label

"Warm red", "bright yellow". You judge how well each label fits your target. That judging is the score.

🥫

The paint inside

The real color you pour out. This is the Value — the payload that actually mixes into the cup.

🥤

A full cup

The amounts you pour must add up to one full cup — no more, no less. That "must add to a full cup" rule is softmax.

A jar with a great-matching label gets poured a lot. A jar with a weak label still gets a small splash. You walk away with a weighted blend — a mix, not a single color. That blend is the new meaning of the word.

⚠️
Where the paint picture breaks down

Paint mixing goes one way — you make one cup. But here, every word mixes its own blend at the same time, all in parallel.

The four steps

Here is the recipe, one card per step. Read them top to bottom — each step feeds the next.

1
SCORE — how well does each Query match each Key?

For every word, compare its Query against every other word's Key. A good match gets a high score; a poor match gets a low one. Behind the scenes this match is a dot product: multiply the matching numbers and add them up.

2
SHRINK — make the scores a little smaller

Divide every score by the same fixed number to scale them down. This keeps the next step gentle so it does not over-react to one big score. The full reason for this comes in Module 4.

3
SOFTMAX — turn the scores into percentages

Run the scores through softmax. Messy scores become clean percentages that add up to 100%. A bigger score gets a bigger slice of the cup.

4
BLEND — mix the Values by those percentages

Pour each word's Value into the cup using its percentage. Add them up. Out comes a brand-new set of numbers — the word's fresh, context-aware meaning.

💡
Routing versus payload

Steps 1–3 decide how much to pour (the routing). Step 4 decides what gets poured (the payload). The matching and the mixing are two separate jobs — that split is the heart of attention.

Softmax, in plain words

Softmax is the step that worries people the most, so let us make it boring. It takes any list of numbers and does two things, every time, with no exceptions:

💯
The percentages always add up to 100%

No matter what numbers you feed in, the slices fill exactly one full cup.

📈
A bigger score always gets a bigger slice

Softmax never flips the order. The strongest match keeps the largest share.

Here is the real example you will keep seeing in this course. The word "sat" scores the two words "cat" and "sat". After shrinking, softmax turns those scores into these slices:

So "sat" listens about two-thirds to itself and one-third to "cat" — and those two slices, 33% and 67%, add up to a full cup. That is softmax doing its only job.

The same recipe, written as one formula

Everything you just learned has a one-line name in math. You do not need to read it like an equation — read it like a label that points at the four steps you already know.

🧮
The attention formula

Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax( Q Kᵀ / √dₖ ) · V

Read left to right, it is the recipe. Here is what each piece is doing.

Q Kᵀ Step 1 — SCORE. All the match scores: every Query compared against every Key at once.
÷ √dₖ Step 2 — SHRINK. The gentle shrink: divide the scores by a fixed number so the next step stays calm.
softmax( … ) Step 3 — SOFTMAX. Turn the scores into percentages that add up to 100%.
· V Step 4 — BLEND. Pour and mix the Values (the payloads) by those percentages.
