Module 4 · Day 4 · play with it

Drag the head count — and watch the views multiply

One word’s vector is 8 slots wide. Split it into h heads and each head gets a thinner slice, d_k = 8 ÷ h. Drag the slider through 1, 2, 4 and 8 heads and watch the slices recolour and the heads switch on.

One vector, h slices

h = 2 heads · each head is d_k = 8 ÷ 2 = 4 dims wide · 2 × 4 = 8 → same total width, so about the same cost as one big head, but 2 different views.
What to watch. Set it to 1 head first: the whole vector is one colour and you get a single view — one relationship, blurred together. Now step up to 2, then 4, then 8 and two things happen at once: the long vector splits into that many colored slices (each one thinner — that is d_k shrinking), and that many heads switch on, each free to specialize in a different relationship. Extra views cost width per head, never width overall.
