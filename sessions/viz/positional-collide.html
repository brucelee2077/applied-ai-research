Each row is one seat's stamp. Two rows that look the same are the same — that is a collision.
A built-in trap. The word the sits in seat 0 and again in seat 4. Same word, two seats — the only thing keeping them apart is the stamp. Hit just one wave and compare those two rows: they go identical too. The red outline can only mark one pair at a time, so it names the closest one — but once the waves are this bad, seats collide all over the sentence.
