What to notice

  • A single neuron is one line. XOR's two classes sit on opposite diagonals, so no single line can separate them — the best you ever do is 3 of 4. XOR is not linearly separable.
  • Add one hidden layer and the network draws two lines, then combines them. Two lines can fence off the corner region where exactly one input is on — now all four points are correct.
  • This is the whole reason for depth and non-linear activations: stacking layers composes simple lines into curved, carved-up regions that a single neuron could never express.
