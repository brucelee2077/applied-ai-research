NumPy Broadcasting

What am I looking at? Broadcasting lets NumPy add or multiply two arrays of different shapes without writing loops. Pick a shape for each array below. NumPy lines the shapes up from the right and checks each pair of dimensions: they match if they are equal, or if one of them is 1 (that side then stretches to fit). What to notice

  • Align from the right. NumPy lines the two shapes up by their last dimension. A shorter shape is treated as if it has extra size-1 axes added on the left.
  • Each pair must be equal, or one must be 1. If both numbers match, they are fine. If one is 1, that axis stretches to match the other. Any other case is a mismatch and NumPy raises an error.
  • A size-1 axis stretches by repeating its single row or column so both arrays reach the same shape. If any pair fails the rule, broadcasting fails.
