Activations · leaky ReLU

Tilt the flat side and watch a dead unit come back

What am I looking at? The green line is the bend max(αz, z). The dashed red line is plain ReLU, whose negative side is perfectly flat. Slide α (alpha) and watch that flat arm tilt. The circle at z = −3 is a unit that got shoved under zero: when the slope there is 0 it is stuck forever, and the moment the slope is even a little above 0 it gets a nudge back up.

What to notice

  • At α = 0 the negative arm lies flat on the axis: the slope readout is exactly 0, so training has nothing to grip and the marked unit can never climb back. That is a dead ReLU.
  • Nudge α to 0.01 and the output barely changes — −3 becomes −0.03 — but the slope is no longer 0, so the unit gets a small push every step and can recover.
  • Turn α up to 0.1–0.3 and the bend gets gentler and gentler. Push it all the way to 1 and there would be no bend left at all — just a straight line, back to the collapse.
