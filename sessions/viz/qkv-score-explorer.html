Change the question — and watch who wins the match

In the sentence "the animal crossed the river bank", the word bank asks a question. Its Query has four slots — water, place, money, action. Drag those four sliders and watch the match score against each other word redraw. One score = multiply slot by slot, then add the pile.

Four sliders, three scores

"bank" is asking about…
Q = [1.0, 0.5, 0, 0] → scores river 5.4 · bank 1.0 · crossed 0.4. Now drag money up to 1.0 and water down to 0 — the winner flips to "bank" itself, and you've just turned a river-bank into a money-bank by hand.
