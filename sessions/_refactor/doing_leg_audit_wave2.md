# Doing-leg adversarial audit — m05a / m05b / m06 (wave-2 modules)

Read-only, 2026-08-01. These 20 days were built under the FULL wave-2 discipline (writer +
module-wide adversarial reviewer + orchestrator re-plants, and a cross-day pass on m05a/m06).
They were re-audited because two classes were discovered after they shipped: the five no-op
traps and — decisively — **pattern 10, print/assert divergence**. m05b had never had a
cross-day pass at all.

**Read that last row carefully.** Pattern 10 accounts for essentially every survivor. Strip
it out and these 20 days have roughly four non-print survivors between them — the wave-2
discipline held on the nine classic patterns and the five no-op traps. There was one blind

in m02/m03/m04. The single P1 is in m05b — the one module that never had the pass.

## Cross-day collisions

### m05b-vision-transformer — 9 collisions

- **[P1] mechanism-dropped** (day-02, day-03, day-04) — Day 2 names the ADDED position stamp as THE cure for permutation-invariance and proves it; Day 3 assembles its sequence from tile vectors + [CLS] with no position stamp at all, never says so, and its block is provably blind to tile order.
