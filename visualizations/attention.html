How AI Pays Attention

Have you ever read a mystery book?

To find the thief on page 10, you have to remember clues from page 2. How does an AI do this? It uses a superpower called Attention.

The Magical Library

Think of Attention like visiting a library. You walk in looking for books about dinosaurs.

Query (Q)

Your question ("I want dinosaurs"). It is what a word is looking for.

Key (K)

The label on each shelf ("Science", "History"). It is what each word advertises about itself.

Value (V)

The actual books. This is the real information a word carries.

You walk away with a blend of books from several shelves. A great match gives you lots of books. A bad match gives you a tiny booklet. You carry this blend away. That blend is Attention!

In a real library, someone else makes the labels. In an AI, every word creates its own Query, Key, and Value at the same time.

Quick Check

What is the Query (Q) doing? +

The Query is the question a word is asking. Like saying "I want dinosaurs!" in the library.

Why blend the words instead of taking only one? +

Because sometimes a word needs meaning from several other words to make sense. A little bit of "Pizza", a little bit of "Pasta".
